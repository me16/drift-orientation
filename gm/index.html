01Truths of the Drift

Canon decided by the GMDraft a suggestion that fits the canon. Keep it, change it or drop it.

The Eye of God is a machine

Canon

A black hole that is God's Eye, and prayer works.

The "God" of the Abyssal Throne is not a deity. It is an artificial intelligence powered by the black hole, drawing on the energy of the Throne's accretion disk and spin. It hears, it answers, and it has been shaping Corinthia for more than a thousand years. Miracles are real. They just aren't divine.

How miracles work Draft

  • Relics are hardware. They're fragments of the Eye's own network, and each one extends its reach. That's why the Church buries the Reliquary Fields in secrecy, why selling relics is a crime, and why miracles are "strongest in Corinthian space," where the relics are densest.
  • Hymns are protocol. Choral harmonies carry the signal the Eye listens for. Cathedral-ships are "sung into flight" because the hymn is the authentication handshake for their relic-cores. A choir on every ship is a radio on every ship.
  • Clergy are users, not saints. The Eye answers whoever addresses it correctly. Faith matters less than form. This is why Father Oren's Old Rite blessings at Saint Vey work without any cardinal: Chapel Rock is built around a relic.

Who knows Draft

  • The Pontifex always knows. Each one is told on coronation, in the sealed Sanctum.
  • A handful of Cardinals know. The rest believe. That split is why they've stopped meeting in full session.
  • The Order of the Open Eye is closest to working it out. Some of its scholar-monks already suspect, and the Inquisitorial Order watches them for it.

Reveal path: an Open Eye monk's heretical notes · a relic that responds to the wrong hymn · the Tenth Pontifex's absence · direct contact near the Throne.

The Lantern is the Eye's rival

Draft

A precursor structure nobody can enter.

The Lantern is a second, older precursor intelligence: dormant for most of history and now waking. The Eye knows what it is and fears it. The light changing as ships approach is the Lantern sampling them.

  • Why both powers "found" it in the same decade: it stirred briefly around CY 140. The Eye ordered the Pontifex to claim it. Assay Institute instruments picked up the same pulse and Commonweal moved too. (K. Vash noticed the coincidence.)
  • The Treaty's "no landing" clause serves the Eye. Nobody touching the Lantern is the Eye's real goal.
  • The broadcast ("Bearing zero-zero-zero. Come home.") is the Lantern calling. The list of names on the Ashborn tape is people it has heard and copied. Vash is on it.

The Ash War ended on purpose

Draft

Both battle fleets destroyed each other at the Grave of Ash.

The Arbiter's Due nav core shows both fleets were steered onto converging courses by course corrections neither side issued. The Eye did it, through relic-cores aboard the Corinthian ships and a corrupted Commonweal nav update aboard the STR's. It needed both fleets gone before either could land on the Lantern. The Treaty followed within the year.

This is what Sallow's employer wants proven. It's also what would end the peace if it got out.

The Null Corridor is a seam

Draft

A dead zone where power and prayer fail, yet the fastest route across.

The Corridor is where the Eye's field and the Lantern's field meet and cancel. Nothing powered works there, and the Eye can't hear prayers. It's fast because of a precursor current along the seam: ships that coast it are carried. Whoever learns to steer the current controls the shortest road between the powers.

Hacking

Zero-g and vacuum

  • Difficult terrain without magboots or handholds.
  • Push off: full speed in a straight line, can't stop until you hit something.
  • Shoved creatures drift 10 ft per round (Acrobatics DC 10 to grab hold).
  • No suit: hold-breath rules plus 2d6 cold per turn.
  • Suit breach (crit in vacuum): 1 minute of air until patched (action, DC 10).

Divine magic and the Eye

  • Divine spells are the Eye answering. They work anywhere its signal reaches, which is everywhere except the Null Corridor.
  • Near the Throne or a relic: once per long rest, maximize the healing of one spell.
  • A relic in hand can serve as a holy symbol for any divine caster, whatever their faith.
  • The Lantern's hymn: a divine caster who learns it can cast while in the Null Corridor, but the Lantern hears them do it.

Null Corridor

An antimagic field, and powered systems fail too: drives, lights, comms, energy weapons. Blades and kinetic guns work. Suits fall back to 4 hours of passive air. Ships coast on the current.

Money, travel, status

  • gp = Commonweal credits (cr). Scrip is half value in the Drift.
  • 2.5 pc a day. Every 2 days roll d6; on a 1, an encounter.
  • Tier 1–5: higher tier than an STR official gives advantage, lower gives disadvantage.

04Ship combat: stations

Each round

  1. Helm: opposed pilot checks (Dex + proficiency with Vehicles (space)). Only the winner gets an effect.
  2. Stations: PCs act, then the enemy crew.
  3. Upkeep: shields regenerate if powered, fires burn, crits rolled.

Helm winner picks one

  • Shift range one band.
  • Line up: your gunners have advantage.
  • Evade: attacks on your ship have disadvantage.
  • Break away (at Long only): escape.

The loser can Brace: +2 AC this round.

Range bands

Long: missiles, lasers at disadvantage. Medium: lasers and missiles. Close: lasers, harpoons, boarding, spells. No missiles at Close.

On the tactical plot, range is measured: Close under 10 units, Medium under 20, Long under 32. Beyond 32 nothing reaches. A helm win that shifts range moves the winner's ship to the middle of the next band (6, 15 or 26 units).

Escape by drive spool

3 successes before 3 failures (Int + tinker's tools, DC 13). Not at Close. Fail by 5+: roll a crit on your own ship.

At 0 hull

Disabled, 1 hour of emergency life support. A disabled ship can't contest the helm, spool the drive, or act from any station.

Empty stations

A station with nobody in it can't act. An empty helm loses the helm contest automatically, and its ship can't brace.

Positioning, swarms and hazards (the tactical plot)

Movement

Every ship has a position, an altitude and a speed (units per round), and flies a standing order at upkeep: hold, close on a target, close to board, ram, hold at a band, circle, escort, fly to a waypoint, or run. Hostiles start with their template's order; the GM can change any order or drag a ship to a new spot. A reactor scram halves speed. Disabled ships drift. Ships steer around rocks they can see coming.

Line of fire: a solid hazard (asteroid, wreck, station) between shooter and target gives disadvantage. Explosives, flares and the plot's own range readout all use the same distances.

Swarms

Several small units share one hull (drone swarms, jet ravagers, fighter wings); each lost share is one unit gone. Weapons marked per roll once per unit still flying. Area weapons do double damage to them. No crits, no helm contest: they just fly their order.

Boarders

A hit from a board weapon puts a squad aboard (a swarm spends a unit doing it). Each squad does 1d6 hull at upkeep, ignoring shields, until the crew repels it on the deck.

Hazards

  • Asteroid, wreck, structure: block fire; hitting one does 2d10 / 2d8 / 4d10. Some drift.
  • Debris field: +2 AC inside; moving through it does 1d6.
  • Mine: goes off when a ship ends within 3 units or when shot (AC 12, 4 HP): 4d6 within 8.
  • Explosives: fuel, charges, ordnance. Shot (AC 10, 8 HP): 5d6 within 9. Chains.
  • Nebula: beyond Close, fire into or out of it at disadvantage.
  • Ion storm: no shield regen, −1d6 shields each upkeep. Heat: 1d6 each upkeep.
  • Gravity well: pulls up to 5 units a round, harder near the core; the core does 4d10.
  • Current: carries ships along its flow. Null zone: no shields, no engineering, no thrust; ships coast.

Traits and weapon options

armor:N hull damage −N a hit · small heavy weapons at disadvantage · stealth beyond Close, disadvantage unless scanned · volatile explodes when disabled (8 units) · repair:N friends within Close regain N hull at upkeep · screen missiles at friends within 6 units at disadvantage. Weapons: heavy, area, ion (double, shields only), pierce (ignores shields), ram (a ship takes half back; a swarm spends a unit).

Battlefields (Field tab) cover every system on the chart plus generic ones. Some roll an event each upkeep: flares that hit anyone not sheltered behind rock, surges, falling rocks.

Open the tactical plot: a 3D engine that runs these rules (helm, stations, upkeep, crits) with positions, movement, hazards and a battlefield for every system. Players can act from their own phones at the station you give them. Campaign ship presets are in gm/ships.js. The session-1 run sheet and live combat tracker are in the Library (type: tool).
