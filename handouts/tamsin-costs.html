Vessel owners

  • Food: a Duplicator installed aboard meets the Low tier automatically. A 25 cr/GSM subscription raises it to Average.
  • Accommodations: a registered vessel meets the Low tier. A one-time fit-out of 100 cr raises it to Average, or 500 cr to High.

Tiers are self-declared and may be reviewed. Residents who fall below the Low tier are referred to the Spindle level 2 desk as persons with unresolved obligations. Your satisfaction is not a Commonweal service.
