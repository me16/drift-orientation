# CLAUDE.md

Guidance for working in this repo.

## What this is

A **sci-fi D&D campaign site**, static, with no build step:

- **`index.html`**: the player handout, presented as an in-world artifact: a Commonweal civic datapad the party (or one PC) bought secondhand at Free Harbor Tamsin.
- **`gm/`**: the GM partition (`gm/index.html` + `gm/library.js` + `gm/maps/`), behind a soft password. See "GM partition" below.

Rules for the player handout:

- **No GM secrets in `index.html`.** Mysteries (the Lantern, who owns Commonweal, the Ninth Pontifex's barge, the Ninth Hollow, Kestrel's silence, the Corridor, what the Eye really is) are hinted at, never explained. Secrets go in `gm/` only.
- **Everything is diegetic.** Text is written by someone in the setting (Commonweal, K. Vargas, dockside gossip). No out-of-character rules text in the page itself unless the user asks for it.
- **Commonweal is an unreliable narrator.** It's an STR company, so its "public record" is slanted toward the STR. Keep that bias subtle.

## Voices

| Source | Where | Style |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Commonweal docs | Doc bodies, gazetteer `sum`, glossary | Dry, bureaucratic, short declarative sentences, faintly condescending to non-citizens ("Persons with unresolved obligations.") |
| K. Vargas annotations | `.note` blocks, `NOTES` map | Terse, first-person, wry, a little world-weary. One or two sentences max. |
| Dockside talk | `talk` field, general rumors list | Overheard first-person hearsay. Hooks, not facts. |
| Mottos / scripture | `.motto`, `.lede` | Serif italic. Short. |

## Code map (`index.html`)

- **CSS** (top `<style>`): design tokens on `:root`. Faction colors: `--dom` lilac (Corinthia), `--str` teal (STR), `--drift` amber, `--haz` red, `--lan` pale gold (the Lantern only). Fonts: Michroma (display), Exo 2 (body), Cormorant Garamond italic (lore), Share Tech Mono (data). Mobile rules are under `@media (max-width:760px), (max-height:500px)`.
- **Static docs**: `<section class="doc" data-r="...">` blocks. Routing is hash-based (`route()`, `CRUMBS`). Adding a tab means adding a section, a `nav.files` link, and a `CRUMBS` entry.
- **`DATA`**: one object per gazetteer/chart entry:
  `{id, code, f, name, cls, model, pos:[x,y,z], pop, sum, bodies:[[name, desc]...], talk, svc}`
  - `f`: `dom | str | drift | haz`. `code` prefixes: `COR-`, `STR-`, `DRF-`, `HAZ-`.
  - `pos` is in parsecs. Tamsin-relative range, bearing and days are computed (`navCalc`), never hard-coded.
  - `model` must be a key of `BUILD` (blackhole, station, shipyard, fortress, relics, cityplanet, exchange, array, colony, port, mining, boomtown, deadstar, lantern, fleet, corridor, barge, gasgiant, habitats, foundry, farm). `gasgiant` also needs `colors:[4 hex]` and `moons:n`. Optional `tint` recolors lilac parts.
  - Each entry automatically gets a gazetteer card, a chart node, a label, and a dockside-talk row.
- **`NOTES`**: K. Vargas's per-system annotations, keyed by `DATA` id. The sidebar's annotation count is computed.
- **General rumors** (`GEN-xx`): inline array next to `#rumorList`.
- **`REGIONS`**: floating region labels on the chart.
- **three.js r128** + OrbitControls come from CDNs (cdnjs / jsdelivr). Keep those exact versions; the code uses the pre-module `examples/js` globals.

## Canon numbers (keep consistent)

- Now: **CY 312**. Corinthian reckoning runs ~730 years ahead of CY.
- Drive speed: **2.5 pc/day** (`PC_PER_DAY`). The Drift spans **x = −13 to +13** (26 pc, drawn as dashed lines on the chart). Known space runs about 100 pc west to east (x ≈ −51 to +48). Corinthia is west (−x), the STR east (+x), the Null Corridor north (−z), the Grave of Ash south (+z).
- The Lantern is at the origin. Tamsin is at `[3,0,11]`. The Briefing's facts (Tamsin→Seraphis ≈ 13 d, Tamsin→Meridian ≈ 15 d) are hand-written, so update them if those positions move.
- Key dates: Charter CY 0 · Commonweal CY 31 · contact and Lantern claims CY 140 · Border Wars 141–190 · Tamsin CY 196 · Arbitration Clause CY 233 · Copperline rush CY 251 · Ash War 272–274 · Ninth Pontifex dies CY 273 · Treaty CY 274 · Council of Captains CY 281 · Free Labor League CY 298 · Psalter famine 310–311.
- "Twenty factions" on the Briefing = the sub-faction rows on the Powers tab (6 + 7 + 7). Update that if you add or remove any.

## D&D framing (working assumptions, so confirm with the user before relying on them)

- **Faith = divine magic.** Miracles are "real and repeatable, strongest in Corinthian space." Divine casters (clerics, paladins, Old Rite hedge-priests) are a natural fit.
- **Technology = STR-licensed gear**: firearms, implants, AI, drives. A natural fit for artificers and tech-flavored equipment.
- **The Null Corridor** suppresses both "power and prayer" (an antimagic-and-no-tech zone), yet it's the fastest route across.
- **Origins tab** = background and character-hook prompts. "The touched" is the slot for sorcerers, warlocks and other unexplained gifts.
- System and edition (5e, 5.5e, a sci-fi 5e variant, …) aren't stated anywhere yet. Ask before writing anything rules-specific.

## GM partition (`gm/`)

- **Soft lock only.** The access phrase is checked client-side against a `cyrb53` hash (`PASS_HASH` in `gm/index.html`) and remembered in `localStorage`. Anyone who reads `gm/index.html` or `gm/library.js` can see everything. The GM accepted this; don't present it as secure. To change the phrase, run `gmHash("new phrase")` in the browser console on the GM page and paste the result into `PASS_HASH`. The current phrase is "the eye sees" (case-insensitive).
- **Truths** are tagged `Canon` (decided by the GM) or `Draft` (suggested, open to change). Never promote a Draft to Canon on your own. Current canon: the "God" of the Abyssal Throne is an AI powered by the black hole, not a deity.
- **Library** (`gm/library.js`): a `window.GM_LIBRARY` array the GM edits by hand. The file header documents the fields. Search covers title, type, grid, where, tags, notes and credit; type chips are built from the data.
- **Other creators' maps are linked, not copied.** Use `drive` / `folder` / `url`. Only original work goes in `gm/maps/` (compressed JPG, with a `thumb-` copy around 480 px referenced by `thumb`). One exception, approved by the GM: `gm/maps/pbmi-dropship-patience.jpg` (PBMI DropShip art, the Patience) is copied in so the ship-combat station markers line up with it. Markers are SVG overlays in image pixels (viewBox 1428×2000), so don't recrop or resize that file without moving them.
- **Tonight** (`#tonight`, first section): the plan for the next session. Now session 1 on Saint Vey, **played**: a "What happened at the table" box at the top records the outcome (CAR-B ν_player revealed and wants to join the crew, the deal with Amelda to load Inquisitor Tracking Gems into 30 crates for passage, departure in two galactic standard weeks, rumor 1 now hints Florb lives) and wins over the plan below it. The plan covers waking in the Crescent Fields, the three-day survival walk (gemglams, soil water, exhaustion), New Bombswick and its NPCs, the Platinum Shard, the nav core's blackbox, Amelda Dawntreader at the ferry port (droid fight, Med-Prol strike squad or a deal), and a rough Free Harbor Tamsin sketch. The GM wrote the core of it; anything marked *suggestion* was filled in and is a plan, not Canon. The previous plan (session 0, the Grave of Ash job and crossfire) is folded up in `#session0`. When a new session is planned, fold the current one into a "last session" section the same way.
- The player handout links to the partition from its sidebar footer ("Restricted partition").

## Tactical plot (`combat/`)

- `combat/index.html` runs the GM page's ship-combat rules (`gm/index.html#ships` for stations, ranges, crits and hazards; `#flight` for initiative turns, flight, pilot actions and Edge) in a three.js r128 view. Keep the rules in sync with both sections. Campaign presets are in `gm/ships.js`. `#flight` is still tagged Draft (numbers under playtest): don't promote it to Canon on your own.
- **Live player view:** the GM page publishes a player-safe copy of the fight to Firebase Realtime Database over REST, using anonymous auth. Players open `combat/?watch=CODE`, which follows the room with an `EventSource` stream. Settings are in `combat/live-config.js`, and `database.rules.json` makes only a room's creator able to write to it. When "hide hostile stats" is on, give any new log line that reveals enemy numbers a player version: `log(html, cls, pub)`.
- Anything loaded from a file or from Firebase goes through `sanitizeState()`, and log HTML goes through `safeHtml()`.
- **Crew and seats:** `S.crew` holds the characters and their bonuses (pilot, gun, missile, tech, sensors), plus `npc` for hands the GM runs (players can't claim them, `playerAction()` refuses them; "Crew the empty stations with NPCs" is `npcfill`); `S.seats` maps `"<shipId>:<station>"` (`helm`, `eng`, `sens`, `w<weaponIndex>`) to a crew id. Once the crew list isn't empty, an empty party station can't act (`manned()`). Players claim a character in `players/<uid>` and send actions to `actions/<uid>`. The GM page applies each one with `playerAction()` (one action per station per round on its ship's turn, tracked in `S.acted`; one reaction per station per round, in `S.react`) and answers in `replies/<uid>`. Player hits wait in `S.pending` until they enter their damage roll. `lvl` on a crew member sets the pilot's Edge pool (proficiency bonus, d8; d10 from level 11), stored as `c.edge`.
- **The tool's rules match `gm/index.html#ships`:** ship hull values (halved from the first draft), crits on a natural 20 and at 75/50/25% hull (`marks`), and empty or disabled stations can't act. Change both places together.
- **Turns:** `rollInit()` fills `S.init` (`[{id, v}]`, highest first) and sets `S.fight`; `cur()` is the ship whose turn it is (`S.turn`). `beginTurn()` resets the ship's turn state (`TURN0`: `moveLeft`, `spent`, `dodge`, `lock`, `about`, …) and `endTurn()` checks tails and breaking away, then advances; wrapping past the last ship runs `endRound()`. Ships joining mid-fight go through `joinInit()`. Ships added from the Ships tab go through `arriveShip()`: the Add row's **Arrives** (`UI.arrive`: `jump` out of hyperspace, `hole` through a wormhole that closes behind it, `here`) picks the log line and the 3D effect (`FX.arrive()`, mirrored to live players as an `arrive` fx event), and **Distance** (`UI.addAt`) overrides the template's `at`. Allies with `at` 20+ or a set Distance arrive that far out on the far side; other allies form up beside the flagship. Pilot actions are `pilotAct()` (`PILOT` lists them; `S.tact` tracks the action and bonus action); Edge is `edgeOf()` / `spendEdge()` / `gainEdge()`. Hostile and allied turns run in `runNpc()` (fly the order, `aiAction()`, `aiBonus()`, `fireShip()`), automatically when `S.autoNpc` is on; its steps are flags in `s.ai` so a pause can resume.
- **Hostile and allied ships are stat blocks** (like 5e monsters; rules in `gm/index.html#npcs`, tagged Draft): no crew or stations; on its turn a ship moves, takes one maneuver from `NPC_ACT`, maybe a bonus from `NPC_BONUS`, and fires each weapon once. `pilotAct()` refuses the party-only stunts for them, and Boost / Call the line are party-only. Their reactions are decided on the spot. `statBlock()` writes one up (Fleet tab and the Add-ship preview, via `previewShip()`); `TRAIT_TEXT` holds each trait's stat-block wording, so add a line there with any new trait. Templates can carry a GM-only `tactics` line (not sent to players). Weapon option `recharge:N` (`w.rch`, `w.down`) is rolled in `beginTurn()`. `S.avgDmg` makes their hits deal `avgOf()` instead of rolling (`npcDmg()`).
- **Layout (for new players):** the turn-order bar (`#tracker`, `renderTracker()`) shows every ship's hull, shields, Edge pips, crits and status; clicking one calls `pickShip()` (same as clicking it on the plot). The ship card (`#dock`, `renderDock()`, ship from `dockShip()`) sits over the plot's bottom left: hull, shields, movement left, action / bonus / reaction, which stations have acted, a `nextHint()` line, and any `S.wait` decision (unless the turn card is showing it). The log is a drawer at the bottom right (`UI.logOpen`). The Turn tab is a setup checklist before initiative, then numbered steps (Move, Pilot action, Crew). Tabs are Turn, Stations, Crew, Ships (`fleet`), Battlefield (`field`). `guideHtml()` is the Quick guide (`? Guide`, opens once per browser on first visit); update it when the turn flow changes. On phones the bar sticks to the top and the card and log flow under the plot.
- **Turn card:** `S.autoNpc` defaults to off (saves before `v` 4 load with it off). Then every hostile or allied turn pops up `#npcCard` over the plot (`cardShip()`, `npcCard()`, `renderCard()`): Move (its order, quick moves, the plot), Maneuver (the AI's choice from `aiPick()` / `aiBonusPick()` is highlighted), Attack (one weapon at the card's target `UI.mtgt`, or Multiattack via `fireShip(s, prefId)`), Let the AI finish (`runNpc()`, which doesn't end the turn while auto is off), End turn. Clicking a foe on the plot retargets it. The pane, the card and its pill share event handlers (`onPanes()`).
- **Decisions:** anything that waits for a person is `S.wait` (`jink`, `rfire` reaction fire, `brake` Hard brake, `follow`), with `sid` = the ship that decides and `tid` = the other one. Nothing else can happen until `resolveWait()` runs; a paused flight carries on from `wait.resume`. Hostile and allied ships decide on the spot. A reaction move in progress (follow, readied move) is `S.rmove`.
- **Movement:** `moveShip()` flies a ship in a straight line: `planPath()` samples the path, charges double inside `slow` hazards, stops short of solid ones, other ships and null zones, and lists events in order: `prov` (leaves a foe's Close range: reaction fire, unless Break lock) and `haz` (a pilot check, only past half Speed this turn; DCs are `HAZ[k].check` and `DC_*`). The move tool's preview runs the same `planPath()`.
- **Positions and range (no bands):** the battlefield is real 3D space. Every ship and hazard has `x, y, z` in plot units (y is up; ships fly between altitude −25 and +25), and every distance is `dist()` in 3D. There are no range bands anywhere, in the rules or the display: each weapon has `rng` (normal), `lng` (long) and `min` in units (beyond normal: disadvantage; beyond long or inside min: no shot), parsed from `"20/32"` plus `min:10` (old `CML dis:L` strings convert via `rangesFromBands()`). Other thresholds are constants: `CLOSE_IN` (10: reaction fire, stealth, nebulae, running dark, spool, repair) and `TAIL_R` (20); breaking away means out of every foe's `reachOf()`. Readouts use `reachAt()` (in range / long range / out of range), `distChip()` and `altTxt()` (▲/▼ height difference). Ships enter `at` units out (templates), spread above and below. Hostile and allied ships fly `s.order` on their turn (`planMove(s, budget)`; `keep` and `orbit` hold `order.d` units, approaching on a slant and orbiting on a tilt); the 3D view draws it as a dashed ghost for the GM.
- **3D view:** the grid is only the altitude-0 datum (each ship has a stalk to it); the focused ship shows its weapon reach as spheres (green normal, amber long). The camera orbits freely and follows the flagship in 3D. The GM's move tool is two clicks: a point on the level plane, then raise or lower it (shift-click flies level); a player's phone taps a point and sets altitude with a slider.
- **End of round (`endRound()`):** disabled ships drift → hazards (`hazardsUpkeep()`: hazard drift, currents and wells (Slingshot skips the pull), collisions, zones, mines) → battlefield events (`rollEvents()`) → shield regen, fires, `repair`, boarders → reset actions and reactions.
- **Templates:** generic ships are in `combat/templates.js` (its header documents every field, trait and weapon option); campaign and faction ships in `gm/ships.js`; the Patience is in `BASE_PRESETS`. Traits and weapon options are parsed by `parseTraits()` / `parseWeapon()`; add a new one in the parser, in `cleanTraits()` / `W_FLAGS`, in the editor help, and in the GM page's "Positioning, swarms and hazards" panel.
- **Rules popups:** hovering an action (long-press on a phone) shows its rules from `TIPS` in `combat/index.html`, keyed by the button's `data-act` / `data-pact` (pilot actions use `PILOT[k].t`; any `title` shows the same way). When a rule changes, update its tip too; a new action button gets a `TIPS` entry.
- **Hazards:** kinds and their numbers are in `HAZ` (combat/index.html); their 3D look is `Scenery.hazard()` in `combat/scenery.js`. A non-solid hazard with `hh` is a flat disc (`2·hh` thick) instead of a sphere; always test containment with `insideHaz()`, never `dist() <= r`. Mines and explosives can be targeted: the target id is the hazard id negated (`UI.tgt < 0`).
- **Battlefields:** `combat/backdrops.js` has generic ones and one per system on the player chart (`DATA` in `index.html`). Add one there when you add a system. Bodies are scenery far outside play (built by `Scenery.build()`); `hazards` are placed in play by "Use with its hazards"; `events` roll at the end of each round and their text is logged for players too, so keep secrets out of it. Spread hazards through altitude and use `hh` layers (ring planes, cloud decks, lanes) so flying over or under is a choice.

## Checking changes

There's no build or test suite. Open `index.html` in a browser and click through every tab, at a phone width too. To smoke-test headlessly, load the page with Playwright, visit each hash (`#briefing`, `#history`, `#factions`, `#chart`, `#gazetteer`, `#origins`, `#glossary`, `#rumors`, `#loc-<id>`), and check that no page errors appear (CDN load failures are expected in a sandbox without network). For `combat/index.html`, the CDNs are blocked in the sandbox: get three r128 with `npm pack three@0.128.0` into the scratchpad and serve `build/three.min.js` and `examples/js/controls/OrbitControls.js` through Playwright `page.route`, launch Chromium with `--use-gl=swiftshader`, then add every preset, apply every battlefield with its hazards, and run a few rounds (roll initiative, run hostile turns, fly the party's ship with quick moves and through hazards, use pilot actions, fire, resolve every `S.wait`, end turns past the end of a round) checking for page errors. For `gm/index.html`, check the wrong phrase is refused, the right one opens the page, a reload stays unlocked, and search and type chips filter the library.
