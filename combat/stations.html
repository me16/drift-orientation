Tailing

On its tail: gunners have advantage on it, its attacks on you have disadvantage, and you can react to follow it. Ends if it breaks free, you end a turn 20+ apart, or it Hard brakes you.

Break away: end your turn out of every hostile's reach, untailed.

When to use what

  • Scan before the gunners fire. Your turn comes on the ship's turn with everyone else, so act first.
  • Stealthy ships beyond 10 units are attacked at disadvantage until someone scans them.
  • Jam the big gun: pick the hostile whose next attack you fear most.
  • Call the line before the pilot dives through debris, a ring plane or a minefield fast.
  • Hail can end a fight without firing a shot. Ask the GM what you hear on the open channel.

Reading the plot

  • Distances are real 3D units. Our lasers reach 20 (32 at disadvantage); missiles 10 to 32.
  • The close-in zone is 10 units around a ship: leaving a hostile's draws reaction fire. Stealth, nebulae and running dark only protect beyond it.
  • From more than 10 units away, fire into or out of a nebula is at disadvantage. Rock or wreck in the line of fire: disadvantage.

Reaction

You have one reaction each round, on anyone's turn: a spell, or whatever the moment needs.

Your crits

1 Cockpit hit: you and the pilot take 2d6 thunder (DC 12 Dex half). Damage control in the cockpit fixes it.

Working against you

  • Target used Evasive flying, or is tailing us: disadvantage.

Reaction: reaction fire

When a hostile pulls out of our 10-unit close-in zone, fire the turret at it, at the point where it leaves. One reaction per round. (Break lock prevents it.)

Mines and explosives can be shot too: a mine is AC 12, 4 HP (4d6 within 8); explosives AC 10, 8 HP (5d6 within 9, and they chain).

Spooling the drive

3 successes before 3 failures and the drive jumps. Not while a hostile is within 10 units. Fail by 5 or more: roll a crit on our own ship.

Reactor strain

If the pilot Burns two turns in a row, you make a DC 12 check on the second. Fail, or nobody in Engineering: reactor scram.

PATIENCE · STATION CARDAnyone aboard

Off station, or wherever it's burning

The round

  1. Initiative once per fight, rolled by the pilot. Ships act highest first.
  2. On our ship's turn: the pilot flies, each crew member at a station takes one action, and everyone off station acts too.
  3. One reaction each per round, on anyone's turn.
  4. End of round: drift and hazards → events → shields → fires burn → repairs → boarders.

What you can do

Fight a fire6 Bunks, DC 12. It does 1d6 hull every round until it's out.
Patch a breach5 Cargo bay, DC 12. When it opens: DC 12 Dex or get pulled toward the hole.
Repel boardersEach squad aboard does 1d6 hull at the end of the round, through shields, until you drive it off.
Cast a spell1 unit on the plot ≈ 100 ft.
Take a seatAn empty station can't act. Move there and take over next turn.

If the ship goes down

At 0 hull the Patience is disabled: no turns, no flying, no stations. One hour of emergency life support.
