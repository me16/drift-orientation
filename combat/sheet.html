Tailing

Win the contest (ties to you) and you're on its tail: your gunners have adv. against it, its attacks on you have disadv., and you can react to follow it up to your Speed when it moves. Its rear arc still fires at you. Ends if it breaks free (its action, ties to it), you end a turn more than 20 apart, it Hard brakes you, or your helm empties.

The round

  • Initiative, once per fight: d20 + pilot's Dex + proficiency (Vehicles, space). Highest goes first for the whole fight.
  • Your ship's turn: the pilot moves up to Speed (split around actions as you like) and takes an action + bonus action. Every crew member at a station takes one action. Crew off-station act now too: fires, boarders, breaches, spells.
  • Reactions: everyone has one per round, on anyone's turn.
  • End of round: drift and hazard forces → battlefield events → shields regen (if powered) → fires burn → repairs → boarders.

An empty station can't act. An empty helm coasts along its last move and can't Brace or Jink.

Range and movement

  • Real 3D distances in units. Lasers 20/32: past 20 at disadv. Missiles 10–32. Harpoons, flak, boarding gear: 10.
  • Close-in zone = 10 units. Pulling out of a hostile's draws reaction fire (one weapon) unless you Break lock.
  • Can't end a move within 2 units of another ship. Solid rock or wreck in the line of fire: disadvantage.
  • Difficult space (debris, ring plane, nebula): each unit costs 2.

Hazard checks

Only if you use more than half Speed and pass through it: d20 + Dex + prof. Hard line: +5 DC, success regains an Edge die.

Getting out

  • Break away: end your turn beyond every hostile's long range, untailed. If none gets you back in reach before your next turn, you're gone.
  • Spool drive: 3 successes before 3 failures, Int + tinker's tools DC 13. Not with a hostile within 10. Fail by 5+: crit yourself.
  • 0 hull: disabled. No turns. 1 hour of emergency life support.
