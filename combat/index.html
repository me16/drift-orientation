Format: Name | +attack | damage | bands | options | note
Bands: Close (under 10 units), Medium (under 20), Long (under 32), e.g. CML. Beyond 32 nothing can fire.
Options: dis:L (disadvantage at Long), missile, helm (fires only if your pilot won the helm), ammo:6, heavy (disadvantage against small targets), area (double damage to swarms), ion (double damage, shields only), pierce (ignores shields), per (damage once per swarm unit), board (a hit puts a boarding squad aboard), ram (a swarm spends a unit; a ship takes half the damage back). The note is shown on a hit.
Traits: armor:N (hull damage −N per hit), swarm:N (N units share the hull; no crits, no helm contest), small, stealth (beyond Close, attacks at disadvantage unless scanned), volatile:3d6 (explodes when disabled, 8 units), repair:N (friends within Close regain N hull each upkeep), screen (missiles at friends within 6 units at disadvantage).
